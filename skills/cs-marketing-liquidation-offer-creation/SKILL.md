# Liquidation offer creation

Turn a real product into a front-end offer a cold buyer can understand, picture
and purchase. Here, liquidation means a low-ticket acquisition offer intended
to offset acquisition cost, not a clearance sale. Do not claim it pays for its
traffic until measured contribution supports that claim.

This skill owns the **mechanism-to-creative offer pack**, not all marketing.
Use `cs-marketing-offer-creation` for broader ICP, pricing ladder and offer
economics work; `cs-marketing-image-ads` for image execution when available.
Do not require a CodeSpring project connection merely to name an offer.

**Maturity: draft.** Derived from the 22 September 2026 App Idea Test Drive
session and founder-selected visuals. The packaged skill has not yet been
independently rerun. “Winning” in that session meant founder preference, not
measured sales, CPA or profitability.

## Start with product truth

Recover these from supplied context; ask only for consequential missing facts:

- Buyer and their immediate decision, anxiety or obstacle.
- What this purchase actually delivers, including limits and fulfilment effort.
- The desired downstream business and why this buyer could fit it.
- Price, destination and approved terms; leave unknowns unknown.
- Existing evidence, original identity assets, branding and selected creative.

Separate `EVIDENCE`, `USER DIRECTION` and `HYPOTHESIS`. A distant aspiration
(stable income, a profitable SaaS) is not automatically this purchase's promise.
An analysis tool can inform a build decision; it cannot prove people will buy
or guarantee virality. A score is an assessment, not a success probability.

## Discover the mechanism through the bookshelf

Read [mechanism-discovery.md](references/mechanism-discovery.md) for this mode.
Use relevant bookstore categories, not just competitors' SaaS names. Waterstones
business/startup/self-employment titles were the inspiration source in the
worked example. Adjacent self-help, finance or wellbeing titles can reveal
clear naming patterns without becoming sources for product claims.

Capture a small useful set of titles with sources, the promise each implies,
its naming pattern and whether that pattern fits the actual product. Extract
the logic, not the title: a test before commitment, a tangible destination,
a bounded task, a repeatable habit, an operating model. Do not copy a title
and change one word as the default naming method.

Generate a shortlist across different promise/mechanism territories. Evaluate:

1. Does a stranger know what they can do with it?
2. Does it answer the buyer's actual concern?
3. Does the product fulfil what the name implies?
4. Can it become a simple image and a matching landing-page headline?
5. Is it distinguishable from known offers? Availability is not legal clearance.

Explain the leading choices briefly. Preserve the user's selection. The goal
is not a clever name in isolation: align **name → promise → deliverable → CTA**.
A metaphor packages a mechanism; also state the actual process behind it.

## Package the purchase

Write one plain sentence for what each genuinely included item does. A bundle
is not permission to invent bonuses. Do not multiply one tool into several
deliverables to make the stack look bigger. Confirm tentative items before
presenting them as sold inclusions. Treat pricing matrices as pricing tools,
not just decorative checkmark tables; ask what calculation or decision they enable.

Capture fulfilment cost, fees, refunds and support/call capacity when evaluating
economics. Break-even acquisition cost is net collected front-end revenue less
variable fulfilment costs; include bumps only with supported take rates. Keep
future downstream revenue separate from measured front-end contribution.
No fixed $27 rule: that was this session's chosen price, not a universal optimum.

## Make the offer visible, then make the ads

For mockups or ads, read [creative-playbook.md](references/creative-playbook.md).
For the reasoning and selected examples from this discovery, read
[app-idea-test-drive-case.md](references/app-idea-test-drive-case.md).

First lock a recognisable product representation. Then create the requested
ad quantity: concept families test different messages or visual explanations;
variants change execution within a family. Three concepts with two variants
means six separate outputs, not a contact sheet or six unrelated offers.

Use available image-generation/editing capability for raster creation and its
applicable skill instructions. Use original photos and UI sources rather than
repeatedly regenerating degraded derivatives. Retain accepted composition;
make only requested changes during revisions.

## Boundaries and completion

Offer/creative work authorises research, local documents and requested images,
not ad spend, publication, customer messaging or live checkout changes. Do not
silently add refund promises, scarcity, performance claims or extra inclusions.
If a guarantee is requested, tie it to a supportable result of this purchase
and explicit refund terms. Do not import a competitor's CPA or revenue guarantee.

Save a concise `OFFER-PACK.md` (or update the user's existing brief) with buyer,
product boundary, evidence status, title research/provenance, selected name and
reason, actual mechanism, promise, bundle, price/terms, downstream bridge,
exact ad copy, source assets, prompt set, output paths and checks. Do not create
redundant manifests when the user declines them. Keep private photos and raw
customer records outside this reusable skill repository.

Before completion, verify copy/offer alignment, actual image dimensions,
full-size and phone-feed readability, original-face likeness, geometry, safe
margins and the absence of invented claims. Preserve originals and save edits
as versions. Record remaining defects rather than calling them verified.

Use `DRAFT` for unresolved offer decisions, `IN PROGRESS` while producing,
`READY` for reviewed deliverables, `AWAITING APPROVAL` for a real decision and
`BLOCKED` for a missing necessary input. `LIVE / VERIFIED` requires authorised
publication and evidence; attractive creative does not establish performance.

Hand the approved pack to `cs-marketing-image-ads` for further production and
`cs-marketing-website` for page alignment. Hand economics to the broader offer
workflow if needed. Do not start these external changes without scope.
