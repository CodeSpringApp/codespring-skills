# Legitimate discovery and referral ledger

Track every external profile, launch, integration, review or editorial mention.

## Qualification

Keep only prospects that have a relevant audience, truthful profile/review/integration value, credible editorial discovery or real community-launch value. Reject sources selling dofollow links, bulk placements, reciprocal exchanges, spun descriptions or fake reviews.

## Required columns

`source | audience/relevance | reason | proposed/referral URL | rel | cost | owner | approval | status | submitted date | live verification URL | referral sessions | conversions | review date`

## Status

Use **DRAFT**, **AWAITING APPROVAL**, **SUBMITTED**, **LIVE / VERIFIED**, or **REJECTED**. A submission or a no-follow link is not automatically failure; assess relevant referral traffic and conversion quality.
