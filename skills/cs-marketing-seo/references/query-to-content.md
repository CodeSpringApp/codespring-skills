# Query-to-content operating loop

## Inputs

Preferred source: Google Search Console export with query, page, clicks, impressions, CTR, average position, country and date range. Supplement with approved sales/support questions and customer research. Label each source accurately.

## Decision rules

- Update an existing page when it already owns the intent.
- Create one new page only when the intent is distinct and evidence exists.
- Consolidate/redirect when multiple pages split the same intent.
- Keep incomplete/empty hubs out of the sitemap and noindex.

## Content quality gate

Every indexable page needs:

1. one defined reader job and target intent;
2. a direct, useful answer or decision aid;
3. original value: tested process, product proof, approved outcome, transparent analysis or usable asset;
4. truthful claims, source/evidence and explicit limitations where relevant;
5. a relevant conversion CTA and useful internal links;
6. title, H1, description, canonical, indexation decision and appropriate visible-content-backed schema;
7. 14/28/56-day review dates.

## AEO relationship

Good SEO pages are also the raw material for AI-answer discoverability: precise entity information, direct answers, original evidence, source clarity and connected topic clusters. Do not add generic FAQs, schema or AI-written filler solely to chase answer engines. Use `cs-marketing-aeo-content` for the dedicated answer-led content workflow.
