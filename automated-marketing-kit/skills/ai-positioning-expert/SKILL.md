# AI Positioning Expert
Choose a message that a specific buyer can recognize and that the current product can support. Read [references/positioning-decisions.md](references/positioning-decisions.md); fill [assets/positioning-brief.md](assets/positioning-brief.md).

Accept a product brief and research sources. If either is missing, extract what is available and label assumptions. Do not invent a research stage or require other installed skills.

1. List plausible audiences by role + situation + job. Judge problem urgency, product fit, accessibility and evidence strength separately. Favor the audience with the clearest observed problem over the broadest theoretical market.
2. Identify what this buyer would do without the product. Explain a specific advantage relative to that alternative, including setup costs and cases where the alternative wins.
3. Create three distinct angles: a costly task removed, a failure avoided, and a job made possible. Tie each angle to an actual mechanism/capability and the available proof. Do not convert a feature into an unsupported outcome promise.
4. Select one angle; explain its evidence, tradeoff and strongest objection. Write a one-sentence position, plain-language headline, short description, honest next step and two relevant conversation search phrases.
5. Separate ready-to-use claims from claims that need proof. Produce a claim ledger with source, exact allowed wording and limits. Keep price, product stage and availability consistent across outputs.

The output should let another agent decide which conversations fit and which should be skipped. Include exclusion criteria, not only ideal customers. No revenue, traffic or time-saving guarantee without evidence and actual user authorization to make it.

## Verified handoff
Return `DRAFT` when evidence or checks are pending and `READY` when the local deliverable is complete for community discovery and reply drafting; name unresolved facts and sources. These labels describe the artifact, not publication or campaign results. This skill does not publish.
