# Humanizer Prompt & Skill Pack
Improve readability without changing the promise. Read [references/voice-and-prompts.md](references/voice-and-prompts.md) when you need a reusable rewrite prompt or a voice worksheet.

1. Identify the actual question, intended reader, platform limits and action. Ask for a short voice sample only if matching a particular person matters; otherwise use clear, direct language.
2. Preserve a fact ledger: capabilities, price, availability, evidence/citations, qualifiers, important limitations and founder affiliation. Do not remove these to make copy sound smoother.
3. Replace abstract claims with concrete tasks and real details already supplied. Cut duplicated openings, inflated adjectives, filler transitions, generic praise and the pitch that does not answer the question. Vary sentence length only where it improves meaning.
4. Put useful advice before the product suggestion. Keep the vocabulary this audience uses without imitating a stranger's identity or inventing personal experience. Natural language does not mean fabricated typos, slang, anecdotes or “undetectable AI”.
5. Return the revised draft plus brief notes on material edits and any claim that needs verification. If a product recommendation is present, preserve clear affiliation. Keep source references close to factual comparisons.

Do not publish, impersonate a customer, hide sponsorship or claim the output passes an AI detector. A truthful plain reply may still sound polished; accuracy takes priority over pretending it was written a particular way.

## Verified handoff
Return `DRAFT` when evidence or checks are pending and `READY` when the local deliverable is complete for owner review; name unresolved facts and sources. These labels describe the artifact, not publication or campaign results. This skill does not publish.
