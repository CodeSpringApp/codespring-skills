# Voice worksheet and ready-to-use prompts
## Voice card
Fill once per founder/product: three own writing samples; typical greeting or no greeting; contractions; usual sentence length; phrases actually used; phrases never used; technical detail level; how you acknowledge limitations; example of a truthful founder disclosure.

## Rewrite a reply
“Edit this reply to answer the original question more directly. Keep facts, citations, product limitations and my affiliation. Remove filler, generic praise and unsupported claims. Do not invent personal experience or add a pitch. Return the edited reply and any facts you could not verify. Original question: … Product facts: … Reply: …”

## Match my real voice
“Use the following examples of my own writing as a voice reference, not a source of new facts. Keep the draft's meaning. Borrow my level of directness and vocabulary, not exact sentences. Preserve disclosures and qualifications. Samples: … Draft: …”

## Shorten without losing trust
“Shorten to fit [platform/length] while retaining the useful step, scope of the claim, key limitation and affiliation if I recommend my product. If these cannot fit honestly, omit the product recommendation rather than the disclosure. Draft: …”

## Make a technical explanation usable
“Rewrite for [role] doing [task]. Replace jargon with what they click, decide or observe. Keep technically necessary terms and explain them once. Mark any claim that needs evidence. Draft: …”

## Repair a sales-heavy reply
“Separate the helpful answer from the sales pitch. Keep one actionable step that addresses the thread. Include my product only if it materially fits and the supplied community rules allow it. Preserve an explicit founder relationship. Otherwise return a helpful no-link reply. Thread/rules/product/draft: …”

## Example — fictional, not a customer result
Before: “Unlock unparalleled productivity with our revolutionary approval solution!”
After: “If approvals keep getting buried in chat, put each request in one queue with an owner and due date. I build QueueLeaf, which handles that queue; it doesn't connect to your accounting system.”
The edit adds a concrete suggestion from the supplied product context, retains a limitation and makes the relationship clear. Do not use the example product as a real recommendation.

## Final check
Would a person understand the next step? Can every claim survive a fact check? Does the message answer this thread? Is the product connection honest? Did the edit imply firsthand experience the writer does not have?
