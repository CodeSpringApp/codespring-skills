# Useful replies, mention choices and authorized publication
## Start with the answer
Read the relevant existing comments and author replies first. Note what is already answered, what the author has clarified and the remaining contribution your draft makes. Do not repeat a popular reply or treat another person's promotion as permission for your own.

Say the one practical thing that addresses the thread. Give a step, decision criterion or relevant alternative. Explain a real tradeoff. A reader should benefit without opening your link. Avoid generic compliments, a long product intro, unexplained jargon and an answer that only tees up your pitch.

Use the founder's actual voice if supplied. Otherwise write ordinary clear sentences. Do not add “bro”, fake typos, invented frustration, customer stories, claims that you tried a tool or an independent-customer persona. Humanizing cannot erase facts, limits, citations or affiliation.

## Choose one route per thread
- **Direct link:** a relevant resource/product meaningfully answers the request and current community/thread rules permit that exact kind of recommendation/link. State “I build [product]” or the real commercial relationship beside it. Verify the URL. A UTM tag does not make a prohibited link acceptable.
- **Profile reference:** use only if it is itself relevant and permitted. Verify the product is actually visible on the profile and check the clickable destination before promising a signup or offer. Flag a stale or mismatched destination; do not edit the profile unless requested. State your product relationship. Never say “link in my profile” to get around a ban on promotion or links. If it functions as promotion, apply the same rules gate.
- **No product mention:** the useful advice stands alone, the product is only tangential, or promotion is not appropriate. A complete no-link answer is a legitimate result; do not plant brand bait.
- **Skip:** no useful truthful answer, poor fit, resolved question, restricted context, outdated discussion, closed thread, uncertain rules or likely repetition. Record the reason rather than forcing a reply.

## Draft card
Thread URL; observed request; source evidence; what existing replies cover and the author clarified; remaining useful contribution; current rule URL/check time; product-fit/limitation; mention route and reason; verified destination; draft; affiliation if relevant; user authorization scope; account/tool availability; prior contact check; status.

For an acquisition campaign, review strong fits for a useful, permitted product recommendation. Include direct links where they help answer the question and profile references where they suit the conversation and real profile destination. No fixed mix is required. An all-no-mention batch can be correct, but explain the actual fit/rules evidence rather than silently removing the campaign's commercial purpose.

## Before publication
Check latest stop/go instructions and existing specific authorization. Confirm text/destination/account/action and quantity fit that scope. Reuse a supported existing session or legitimate configured API. Research/drafts do not require signup/sign-in. If posting is requested and no account/session is available, provide the official steps in [reddit-account-setup.md](reddit-account-setup.md), let the user complete credentials, verification and CAPTCHA, and resume the existing authorized work once access is available. If posting remains unavailable, deliver ready-to-paste text and original thread links, state “not posted”, and stop the mutation. Do not invent passwords, auto-login, connectors or access rights.

Reopen the thread/rules, check prior replies, account permission and any moderation notice. Do not publish if the thread changed or the user issued HOLD. When authorized and permitted, post the exact intended content, save its returned permalink/time and verify it appears where possible. A UI/API timeout means unknown; inspect the destination before retrying. Stop on denial, removal, rate limit or restriction. No bulk unsolicited DMs, repeat pitches, fake accounts, evasion, votes or manufactured engagement.

## Handoff
“Drafted three replies; not posted because this session has no posting access” is accurate. “Automated your Reddit marketing” is too vague to establish what happened. Record real replies/outcomes only. No traffic, ranking, sale or scheduling claim follows from a draft.
