# Conversation research and campaign execution
## Search plan
Recover the existing brief and browser choice. For a normal first pass, aim for 20–40 distinct discovery examples across 3–6 relevant communities and 6–10 useful review drafts, scaled to the user request and actual evidence. Batch independent supported searches, deduplicate cards early, and use native help/question filters where available. Screen obvious mismatches before spending time on full threads. Save the first 3–5 comments while the broader pass continues. Do not wait for an exhaustive community report or posting access before doing the authorized drafting work.

Choose one task and audience per session. Start with 4–6 queries using literal buyer phrasing:
- `site:reddit.com/r/[community] "how do you" "[task]"`
- `site:reddit.com/r/[community] "recommend" "[tool category]"`
- `"[competitor]" "alternative" "[specific limitation]"`
- `"[manual workaround]" "[pain]"`
- `"looking for" "[outcome]"`
Use date filters where available; verify the original date on the opened page. Treat recent as a campaign choice, not a universal threshold. A 6-month-old unanswered thread may be research evidence but a poor reply candidate.

## Read before ranking
Read the original post, relevant replies, author clarifications, thread status and moderation notices. Verify whether the problem is already solved. Record community rules URL and check date. If you cannot see the rules, mark `rules_status=unknown` and do not mark the candidate ready. A question that resembles your keyword but requires an unsupported integration is not a fit.

## Separate discovery, drafts and publication checks
- Research evidence: useful language, objections or alternatives; no outreach intent.
- Candidate: meaningful problem fit but needs rules/context verification.
- Review draft: original post and relevant replies have been read; a specific useful contribution is written. Unknown rules, posting approval or prior contact can still hold publication. State those gaps beside the draft. Known promotion restrictions require no product mention or skip.
- Preflight-eligible: current, relevant and permitted interaction, with thread/account/prior-contact checks complete. The offline helper calls this stricter supplied-data state `draft-ready`; it does not authorize publication or prevent creating earlier review drafts.
Do not call discovered cards verified leads or imply review drafts are ready to post. No minimum number of qualified candidates: zero good matches is valid, but continue a reasonable breadth of discovery within scope before concluding that.

## Draft queue format
Use [review-queue.md](review-queue.md) for the primary browser handoff: one selected conversation beside an editable reply, with navigation and copy/open actions. If the user requests native tabs for manual posting, prepare the requested composers and leave submission to them. Keep detailed evidence/score tables in supporting records. Each record retains observed problem, source excerpt/paraphrase, fit, limitation, rules, score, link choice, affiliation, unresolved checks and actual status. Re-read the record before an agent-performed external action.

## Tracking
Track thread-level IDs, not identities in UTM parameters. Use a campaign slug and internal lead ID. The included UTM helper adds source/medium/campaign/content to a supplied public landing URL. It does not enable analytics, install tags or make a link permitted. Verify destination and tracking with your existing analytics. Count actual replies and outcomes; do not assign an impression estimate from a community subscriber count.

## Review
When a reply gets no response, do not automatically bump, DM or repost. Use observed objections and removals to improve fit and product truth. Stop using a community when the rules or moderator feedback indicate your use is unwelcome. A helpful reply with no product mention may be the appropriate outcome.
