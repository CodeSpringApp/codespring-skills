Calculate qualified rate = qualified / reviewed; draft-to-publish = published / drafted; signup rate = attributed signups / attributed visits. Show “unknown” when the denominator or tracking is missing. A click is not a signup; a signup is not a paying user. UTM data may undercount and does not prove causation.

## Decision
Which problem phrasing received thoughtful responses? Which community should we stop using? What objection needs product work? Choose one variable to change next session. Keep any competitor/customer observations tied to evidence rather than diagnosing from small samples.
